Introduction

The question this thread investigates is kind of broad and narrow by design: whether a self-improving loop over a typed knowledge graph changes how quickly a research practice compounds.1

Earlier work treated memory as just a big pile of flat, undifferentiated text. We take the opposite stance: structure first, retrieval second.2

Lema 2026 reports a similar effect in editorial settings, which we fold in here as a second data point, though their sample is small and we flag it as tentative.3

The remainder sketches the method, then the open questions that scope still leaves unresolved.
